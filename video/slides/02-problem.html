The problem

Running a home is a stream of small, boring, slightly risky tasks.

  • The water bill. The electricity bill that is suddenly double.
  • The dentist recall. The permission slip due Friday.
  • The delivery that needs a signature when nobody is home.
  • The "URGENT overdue invoice" that is actually phishing.

None of it is hard. All of it costs attention, usually in the evening, when attention is scarcest. And the mistakes tired people make are expensive.
