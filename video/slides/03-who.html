Who it's for · why it matters

Busy parents and anyone who runs a household.

Most assistants are one more app to open. Quiet Hours is the opposite: it runs on a schedule, handles what it safely can, and surfaces one decision card when something is genuinely your call, with the reason it did not decide for you.

Three rules
1. Every action passes a deterministic autonomy policy before it runs.
2. Anything the policy will not sign off becomes one tap: Approve · Approve and trust · Decline.
3. Every answer is remembered, so it interrupts you less each week.
